# Worship Word Video: Genuine Link Plan

Last updated: 9 August 2026

The aim is to earn useful, relevant recommendations from churches and refugee-support networks. Do not buy links, submit to bulk directories or exchange links purely for ranking.

## 1. Kairos Housing resource-page link

Best location: the existing **Faith** section of [Kairos Housing Resources](https://www.kairoshousing.org.uk/resources.html), immediately after the Welcome Churches link.

**Status:** Published and publicly verified on 9 August 2026.

### Recommended copy

**Multilingual worship videos**

[Worship Word Video](https://www.worshipwordvideo.org/languages/) is a church tool currently provided without charge. It helps English-speaking and multilingual worship leaders find YouTube worship and hymn videos with on-screen words or subtitles in Farsi, Urdu, Arabic, Portuguese and many other languages, build a service playlist and open a clean projection screen.

Created to help churches without musicians and churches including people for whom English is a second language. Any voluntary donations are directed to Kairos Housing.

Recommended link text: **Find free multilingual worship videos**

Published URL: `https://www.worshipwordvideo.org/`

## 3. Short outreach message

**Subject: Free multilingual worship-video resource for churches**

Hello,

I help run Kairos Housing, a UK charity supporting people seeking sanctuary and those at risk of homelessness. I have also created Worship Word Video, a tool currently provided without charge that helps English-speaking and multilingual churches find YouTube worship and hymn videos with on-screen words or subtitles in many languages, including Farsi, Urdu, Arabic and Portuguese.

The tool is designed for churches welcoming people for whom English is a second language. It includes simple playlists and a clean second-screen projection view. We do not host or copy songs or lyrics; the tool links to and embeds videos published on YouTube.

Would you be willing to review it and, if you find it genuinely useful, include it on your relevant worship or sanctuary resources page?

Main site: https://www.worshipwordvideo.org/

Language collections: https://www.worshipwordvideo.org/languages/

Thank you,

Stephen Norcross

## 4. Suggested church-newsletter paragraph

Worship Word Video is a free finder for churches looking for YouTube worship and hymn videos with on-screen words or subtitles. Its multilingual collections cover Farsi, Urdu, Arabic, Portuguese and many other languages, with simple playlists and a clean projection view for services. Visit https://www.worshipwordvideo.org/.

## 5. Tracking and quality rules

- Record the organisation, contact date, response and resulting page URL.
- Personalise every message and contact only organisations whose audience will benefit.
- Ask for editorial review, not a guaranteed link.
- Prefer a direct link to the most useful page; do not force keyword-heavy link text.
- Do not submit the same text to large numbers of sites.
- Recheck live links and descriptions every six months.
- Treat referral traffic, returning churches and catalogue feedback as more important than raw link count.
